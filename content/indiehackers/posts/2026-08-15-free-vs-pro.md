Hey IH —

Following up on Forge Regex (plain English → working regex): https://regex.ststudio.top

I’m still early and want a reality check on the freemium split.

## Current pricing
- Free: 5 AI generations per day (no signup required to try)
- Pro: $9/mo via Paddle — unlimited generations + shareable links + history

Live testing, replace preview, and multi-language export stay free. I’m trying not to cripple free into a fake demo.

## Why this shape
1. Regex is occasional for many people — if free is “unlimited forever,” upgrades die.
2. 5/day is enough to solve a real task today, not enough to live in the tool all week.
3. Share links felt like a clear Pro boundary (public pages + storage), not a fake paywall on core UX.

## What I’m unsure about
- Is 5/day too generous or too stingy?
- Would you prefer a higher free cap + higher Pro price?
- Does “share links” feel worth paying for, or should Pro be VS Code / teams / private history?

If you’ve shipped freemium solo tools: what would you change first at my stage?
